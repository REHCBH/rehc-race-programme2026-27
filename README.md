# REHC Silk Designer

A single-page tool for owners registering new racing colours with the Rashid Equestrian & Horseracing Club.

Owners choose body, sleeve and cap colours and patterns, see a live preview with a written description, and download a design sheet (PNG) or the silks artwork (SVG) to submit to the Racing Director.

## Rules it follows

- Patterns: Rules of Racing, Annexe 6, Figures 4 to 6 (44 body, 17 sleeve, 12 cap patterns).
- Article 34.1: shows the date the registration would run to (30 September).
- Article 34.4: optional second cap, which must differ from the main cap.
- Article 34.5: warns when the colours are all black.
- A pattern can't be the same colour as the area behind it.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app. The silks template, patterns and colours are built in, so nothing else is needed.

## Updating

Replace `index.html` through **Add file → Upload files** and commit. GitHub Pages refreshes within about a minute.

## Open items for racing control

- Annexe 6 repeats some names: body "Diamonds" (pp.78 and 79), body "Hoop" (twice on p.79), sleeve "Diamonds" (twice on p.81). The app tells them apart by page number until official names are set.
- The second Birdseye (p.80) appears in the body figure but is drawn on the sleeves. It is listed under sleeves.
- "White" in the Annexe is shown as "Plain".
- Pattern positions are translated from the Annexe's angled drawings to the front-view template and need sign-off.
- Phase 2: duplicate check against the colours register.
